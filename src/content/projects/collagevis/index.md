## Motivation

<!-- The story of why this project began — the problem indie filmmakers face when
previsualizing scenes without a crew, locations, or budget. Replace this text. -->

Indie filmmakers rarely have the cast, crew, or locations to test how a scene will play
out before the shoot. CollageVis explores how everyday video clips can stand in for that
missing production — a fast, low-cost way to *see* a scene before making it.

## Approach

CollageVis builds a scene from stacked video layers. Each actor clip is automatically
segmented so it can be repositioned, tagged with a name, and face-swapped into a role.

## Evaluation

A study with six indie filmmakers showed that CollageVis supports flexible and expressive
previsualization, letting them iterate on staging and camera quickly.
